ArchivoLATAM – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

ArchivoLATAM ("the App") is an internal desktop tool that publishes the ArchivoLATAM news channel's own videos to its own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access

When the channel owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • an access token that allows the App to upload and publish videos to that account.

2. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect, read or store any data from other TikTok users, viewers or followers. It does not read messages, comments, contacts or analytics.

3. Storage and security

The access token is stored only on the channel owner's computer and is used solely to publish the channel's videos. It is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the App is authorized. Revoking access from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions > Manage app permissions) invalidates the token, and the local copy is deleted.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used by the public.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version will always be available at this address.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hommiestore/archivolatam/issues.
