ArchivoLATAM

ArchivoLATAM is the internal publishing tool of the ArchivoLATAM news channel. Every day the channel produces short animated news videos about Latin America (Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panamá, Colombia, Venezuela, México, Perú and Argentina) and this tool publishes them to the channel's own accounts.
